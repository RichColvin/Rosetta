Cabling Limit Switches or a Pause Button to Rosetta

On this page

  • Connector
  • Cable Sizing
  • Wiring the Switches
  • Limit Switch Notes

GX-12/2 Plug

Connector

A GX-12/2 female plug is used to connect limit switches or a pause button to the Rosetta system. This plug is sometimes called an aviation connector.

Cable Sizing

The cable between the Rosetta system and your limit switch or pause button should:

  • Have 2 conductors within the cable
  • Be stranded - this is more flexible than solid wire
  • Be 22 AWG (gauge; 24 AWG is the minimum) - this is easily soldered to the GX-12 connector
  • Be shielded, with the shield connected to ground at the Rosetta box end only - this will help isolate the signal from noise which could be encountered in adjacent cables (e.g., for stepper motors)

Wiring the Switches

GX-12 Pin Wire Color Purpose
1 Red Signal (to the Rosetta input) - do not connect to any power source
2 Black GND (ground)

Limit Switch Notes

Limit switches are typically wired as normally closed (NC), and the circuit becomes open when the switch is triggered.

If you wire them as normally open (NO), there is a setting in the configuration program you will need to use to make this work as you expect. Do note that this selection applies to all limit switches; you cannot have some wired as NO and others NC.

For NO switches, the switch simply connects the two pins together when it is not triggered. NC switches connect the pins together when triggered. In either case, the Rosetta box supplies the signal itself, so never connect either wire to a power supply.

Each jack takes either:

  • one switch, for one end of an axis's travel (set the jack to min or max), or
  • two switches wired in series, one at each end (set the jack to both ends).

To protect both ends of an axis and still let LinuxCNC tell which end was hit (for example, each end of a curvilinear slide), use two jacks - one set to min and one to max.

One limit jack can take an external Pause button instead of a limit switch, wired the same way - see Pause Button.

Route limit switch cables away from stepper motor cables where you can, and cross them at right angles rather than bundling them together, so electrical noise doesn't cause false trips.

The use of such a switch must be configured on the Inputs, Outputs, and Sounds tab of the Rosetta Settings program.

Setting up Wi-Fi


WiFi Icon

To setup Wi-Fi on your Rosetta system, click on the Wi-Fi icon located on the title bar at the top of the system. It is the one indicated on the image to the right.

From there, you setup Wi-Fi just as you do on other computers.

Links marked ↗ open on the Internet. Without a connection, use another computer or a phone. The latest version of this manual is at https://rosetta.colvintools.com/UserManual/.

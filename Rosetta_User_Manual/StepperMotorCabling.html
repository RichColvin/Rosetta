Cabling Stepper Motors to Rosetta


GX-16/4 Plug

Cable Connector

The connector used to attach a stepper motor to the Rosetta system is a GX-16/4 female plug. This plug is sometimes called an aviation connector.

Cable Sizing

The cable between the Rosetta system and your stepper motor should:

  • Have 4 conductors within the cable
  • Be stranded - this is more flexible than solid wire
  • Be 20 AWG (gauge) - This will ensure the wire can easily handle the amperage for the NEMA 23 stepper motors, and is easily soldered to the GX-12 connector
  • Be shielded, with the shield connected to ground at the Rosetta box end only - this will help isolate the signal from noise which could be encountered in adjacent cables (e.g., for stepper motors)

ℛ Help

The ℛ Help tab on Rosetta’s main screen gathers the manuals and websites you may need while using Rosetta. Click a book to open it in the web browser.

The User Manual, the Gcode Library and the Ornamental Turner’s Handy Book are installed on your Rosetta, so they open without an Internet connection, and each update brings their latest version. The others are websites and need an Internet connection; if your Rosetta isn’t connected, use another computer or a phone.


Rosetta Library (the Rosetta banner) – the Rosetta website, rosetta.colvintools.com, with all of Rosetta’s documentation. Needs the Internet.

User Manual – this manual: how to set up, configure and use Rosetta. On your Rosetta. Also opened by the Rosetta User Manual icon on the desktop and in Applications > Rosetta.

Gcode Library – Gcode programs for the ornamental turner, each explained, ready to download into your Gcode folder. On your Rosetta. Also opened by the Rosetta Gcode Library icon on the desktop and in Applications > Rosetta.

Gcode Quick Reference – LinuxCNC’s list of Gcode commands, for writing or changing your own programs. Needs the Internet.

User Forum – the forum for questions and for sharing ideas with other Rosetta owners. Needs the Internet.

Ornamental Turner’s Handy Book – a short reference (PDF) for the ornamental turner. On your Rosetta.
